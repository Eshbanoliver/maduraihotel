Hotel Madurai

Hotel Madurai is a business class hotel at Udaipur which pampers you with all the facilities and amenities a 21st century traveler expects. The hotel offers guests fine accomodation, business and recreation facilities at a very affordable price i.e. A/C Rooms, cable/satellite TV, private bath with hot and cold shower and room service. With cozy interiors, Hotel Madurai features a polite and welcoming staff working for you in a family-run environment, will be happy to give you good tips on nearby restaurants and tourist spots. You will feel right at home when you try our rich continental breakfast, which may be enjoyed in our cozy breakfast room. Enjoy your stay at Hotel Madurai.
